# 18 — DECISION & CHANGE CONTROL

## PO Decision
Một quyết định phải có:
- Decision ID.
- Date.
- Context.
- Decision.
- Reason.
- Affected scope.
- Source.
- Owner.
- Result.

## Change proposal
- Problem.
- Current baseline.
- Proposed change.
- Why needed.
- Impact on UI.
- Impact on business.
- Impact on data.
- Impact on security.
- Impact on locked scope.
- Test impact.
- Rollback/recovery.
- PO Decision.

## Quy tắc
Không sửa baseline chỉ vì implementation gặp khó.
Nếu có conflict:
`CONFLICT / UNRESOLVED → STOP → PO DECISION`
